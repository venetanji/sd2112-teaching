# Week 5 thesis figures

Used with Gio's request to adapt his thesis for SD2112. Source: Giovanni
Lion, *Concept Formation in Computational Creativity*, public HTML edition
at https://giovannilion.link/thesis/ . These are historical study artifacts,
not new image generations or evidence of current classroom model behavior.

- `week05-thesis-storefront.jpg`: RGB JPEG conversion of
  https://giovannilion.link/thesis/figures/store1.png (1417 x 1063;
  original alpha uniformly opaque). Chapter 5, Figure 5.1, study photograph
  credited there to Miller and Lion (2022).
- `week05-thesis-storefront-crop.jpg`: teaching crop of that same source,
  pixel box `(250, 190, 1000, 753)`. This demonstrates framing, not a second
  exposure, background removal or an image-model edit.
- `week05-thesis-fastgan-1.jpg` and `week05-thesis-fastgan-2.jpg`: original
  bytes from https://giovannilion.link/thesis/figures/storegen1.jpg and
  https://giovannilion.link/thesis/figures/storegen2.jpg . Chapter 5,
  Figure 5.3, first-iteration FastGAN samples; native 128 x 128 resolution.
  The displayed study photograph is one example, not their sole training input.
- `week05-thesis-two-circles.jpg`: RGB JPEG conversion of
  https://giovannilion.link/thesis/figures/twocircles.png (1024 x 1024).
  Chapter 6, Figure 6.1, four historical Stable Diffusion samples for
  the prompt "two circles". Their limitations are not a universal claim
  about all text-to-image systems.

The comparison's two exact circles are drawn separately from explicit
instructions in `deck/week05_mediation_figures.py`; they are not claimed
as a photograph or an image-model output. Theory and case citations are
also retained in the five slides' speaker notes.
