# Week 5 case-image provenance

These are saved course-team co-creation artifacts, not newly generated examples
or a controlled experiment. The clean mark is the shared source for a series of
selected and refined image interpretations.

- `week05-logo-original.png`: the clean source mark already in the teaching deck.
- `week05-logo-before-brightening.png`: saved option 4,
  `sd2112-ai-logo-chalk-circle-big-4.png`, from the 28 September 2026 exploration.
- `week05-logo-brighter-edit.png`: the requested brighter-forest edit of that
  same option, `sd2112-ai-logo-chalk-circle-big-4-brighter-forest.png`.
- `week05-logo-forest-exploration.jpg`: the existing JPEG export of the brighter
  variant; it is retained on the original comparison slide, but is not used as
  the before-image in the new three-stage comparison.

The new PNG copies preserve decoded pixels from the saved artifacts and omit
generation/workflow metadata. No new model call or background removal is used.
The versions illustrate a changing brief and human selection, not final logo
approval or independent model authorship. The accompanying video discussion
identifies V9 as delivered for review; it does not claim human final acceptance.
