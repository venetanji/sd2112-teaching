### Official standard actually read

Hong Kong Intellectual Property Department, Copyright (Amendment) Ordinance 2022, [FAQ 16-18](https://www.ipd.gov.hk/en/copyright/legislative-proposals-and-amendments/copyright-amendment-ordinance-2022/details-faqs/index.html), retrieved 22 October 2026 from raw HTML `div#a16`, `div#a17`, `div#a18` (the lightweight extractor omitted the bodies). Task-local exact body transcript: `evidence/rights-faq16-18-20261022.txt` adjacent to this checkout.

- FAQ 16 requires all circumstances, particularly purpose/nature (including non-profit/commercial character), nature of work, amount/substantiality relative to the whole, and effect on the potential market/value.
- FAQ 17 describes section 39(1): criticism/review of the work or another work; authorized public release/communication; sufficient acknowledgment identifying **title and author**. The impracticability qualification is not needed here: acknowledgment is practicable.
- FAQ 18 describes section 39(2): quotation includes images/screenshots and scholarly work, blogs and social media; public release, fair dealing, a specific purpose, no more than required for that purpose, and sufficient acknowledgment where practicable. Public availability is not itself disqualifying, but does increase exposure and requires this separate scope assessment.
