Authors/title/year/version/page/figure are identified across visible slides, native notes, references and this ledger. ReAct and Model Cards titles are in their source links/headings; Sharma full title/authors in reference39.
- **Residual uncertainty:** this is an evidenced contextual reliance on an exception, not legal advice, a permission grant or worldwide clearance. Cross-border public hosting and later use can change the analysis. Preserve this uncertainty; reassess before a broader main release, commercial reuse or changed amount/purpose. If institution/rightsholder guidance rejects this basis, obtain permission or replace the affected raster excerpts with labelled original explanatory material.
