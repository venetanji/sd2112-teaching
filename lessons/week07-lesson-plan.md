## Activity guidance

**Step 1:** suggested five minutes to form/register, ten to select/describe, five to submit/check. A useful scaffold is: "For [person] in [situation], our [product] uses AI to [action/decision], helping them [benefit]." State what the person still decides and one assumption. TAs help ungrouped students from the start. Do not require three developed alternatives or a separate selection/critique round.

**Step 2:** suggested fifteen minutes to make/discuss, five to upload/reopen. Show a person, product, AI action and human choice in one image; a logo alone does not communicate the product. The same representative includes the same team name visibly. A rough concept image is enough; no coding, model training, video or working prototype. Record tools and supplied assets honestly in private working notes. Optional feedback fits remaining time without adding an output.

Name the activity eyebrows for the existing `attach_reports` hook so the three real ClassPoint report links can be attached after teaching (one question inbox and two team outputs). Do not fabricate report IDs or commit submissions.
