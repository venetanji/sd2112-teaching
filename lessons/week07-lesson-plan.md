## Activity guidance

1. **Team formation:** share interests and contributions. Roles are flexible, not compulsory fixed job titles. Students without teams should approach a TA before the seeding activity; handle names and membership privately.
2. **Registration demo:** use the actual Canvas interface and group set. Students create one team and ensure every member joins it. Do not invent navigation steps, IDs, screenshots or permissions in the slides. Complete registration now if possible and verify again at the end.
3. **Idea seeding:** five minutes alone, ten sharing, ten developing alternatives. Existing Week 4 brainstorms may be revisited but are not binding. A domain such as repair is only the beginning: identify a person, moment and useful product action. The three example seeds are hypothetical, not validated solutions or real student work. Do not collect real sensitive data to test them.
4. **Selection:** compare specific value, a credible AI role, a sketchable interaction and a manageable research direction. Ask whether a simpler rule would suffice. This is a discussion scaffold, not a new scoring scheme. Keep unused alternatives as private working notes.
5. **Development:** an optional sentence is: "For [person] in [situation], our [product] uses AI to [action/decision], helping them [benefit]." An optional sketch is person -> input -> AI action -> result -> human choice. Distinguish a hoped-for benefit from observed evidence. Ask about permission for possible inputs, uncertainty, rejection/undo and one boundary or fallback, without demanding a completed ethics analysis today.
6. **Local pitches:** pairs of neighbouring teams exchange sketches and one useful objection. Whole-room individual pitches are not required for approximately 114 students. No vote, ranking, ClassPoint attention check or extra critique upload is added.
7. **One collection:** deliver one provisional product concept per team through the demonstrated Canvas destination. A short description or rough sketch suffices; no fixed word count, page count or prototype. Reopen the concept and team to verify success. If access fails, ask a TA and record the blocker privately; do not claim success, create duplicate groups or redirect submissions into a public repo.
