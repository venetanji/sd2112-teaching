## Case C1 / 2018 Mountain View

- Primary: NTSB, *Collision Between a Sport Utility Vehicle Operating With Partial Driving Automation and a Crash Attenuator*, NTSB/HAR-20/01 (2020), https://www.ntsb.gov/investigations/AccidentReports/Reports/HAR2001.pdf . Full 96-page PDF downloaded and selectively read 12 November 2026; executive summary pp. ix-xi; investigative pp. 1-6, 12-16, 20, 40; conclusions/probable cause pp. 58-59. PDF positions differ from printed page numbers. The vehicle was a 2017 Tesla Model X P100D with Autopilot TACC and Autosteer, Level 2 partial driving automation, **not FSD (Supervised)** and not autonomous driving.
- Vehicle log: active Autopilot; Autosteer input about 5.9 seconds before impact; lead vehicle lost about 3.9 seconds before impact; no recorded collision warning/automatic braking; impact about 71 mph. Torque not detected during final approximately six seconds (this is not proof of the exact hand position). NTSB reports no hands-on alert in final 60 seconds despite intervals with no detected torque; earlier trip had alerts. Driver could intervene mechanically but that does not establish timely, meaningful oversight. Road map in deck is an original schematic reconstructed from report pp. 1-6/Figure 5, **not to scale**; no actual accident photograph used.
- NTSB probable cause: Autopilot steering into the gore owing to system limitations and driver lack of response due to likely distraction/overreliance; ineffective engagement monitoring contributed. Damaged crash attenuator, unrepaired after prior impact, contributed to injury severity. NTSB recommendations address Tesla's domain safeguards/monitoring and government oversight. The investigation is not a legal liability ruling or a population crash-rate estimate. Company warnings described in the report (pp. 14-15) were not proof of a sufficient design; later software changes (p. 16) must not be projected backwards.
- Civil disposition corroboration: Guardian/Reuters, 8 April 2024, https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2024/apr/08/tesla-crash-lawsuit-apple-engineer . Report describes a settlement in the family's wrongful-death lawsuit just before trial, undisclosed terms. No jury finding in that case; family allegations and Tesla's defence are not NTSB findings. The original court filing/docket and any post-April-2024 procedural developments were **not** independently retrieved. As of the authoring date, the last corroborated civil status here is the 8 April 2024 reporting; do not present this as a docket search proving a 2026 status.
- Design transfer: suggested activation checks, attentive supervision, safe fallback, accessible challenge/review route are **illustrative hypotheses**; the NTSB recommended limits and better monitoring, but this deck does not claim a particular counterfactual prevents this crash. No victim-blaming or broad generalization from one case.

## Experiment R1 / wrong advice

- Cecil, Lermer, Hudecek, Sauer & Gaube (2024), *Scientific Reports* 14:9736, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-024-60220-5 . Published open full paper reviewed in earlier Week 12 revision; targeted methods, findings and image details retained and rechecked against existing source ledger 12 November 2026. Five German online experiments, N=1,403 students and HRM employees; 16 fictional CVs per person against criteria. About 75% advice correct; no-advice and explanation/source variants. Outcome: immediate correct/incorrect suitability decisions; confidence and perceived advice quality separate. Incorrect advice trials lowered task accuracy; tested heatmap/chart explanations did not clearly mitigate overreliance. Some overall advice effects differed; no real employment consequence or universal explanation inference.
- Figure 6a (published PDF p. 9), Experiment 2a only, N=328 overall/214 advice recipients. Blue/pink pool explanation conditions by advice accuracy; orange/green pool advice accuracy by explanation; **not four arms**. Dashed 70.61% no-advice baseline; error bars standard errors. Source crop/CC BY 4.0 licence/sha documented in `deck/assets/week12/provenance.md`. No invented experiment data.
