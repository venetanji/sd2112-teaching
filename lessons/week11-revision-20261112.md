## Suno: what is established and what remains unknown

- 24 June 2024: [original US complaint](https://www.riaa.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Suno-complaint-file-stamped20.pdf), Document 1, case 1:24-cv-11611, directly read 12 November 2026. Plaintiffs are Universal (UMG Recordings and Capitol), Sony Music Entertainment and six Warner entities. They allege unauthorized copying of sound recordings for training and present output examples; these are allegations, not findings.
- 1 August 2024: Suno's [answer indexed as Document 28](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/files/2024/08/SUNO-response-to-copyright-suit.pdf) returned 403 on direct retrieval. [Reuters' same-day account](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/music-ai-startups-suno-udio-slam-record-label-lawsuits-court-filings-2024-08-01/) corroborates Suno's contested US fair-use position. Do not call this a judicial endorsement or claim the full answer was read.
- 25 November 2025: [Warner's own statement](https://www.wmg.com/news/warner-music-group-and-suno-forge-groundbreaking-partnership), directly read 12 November 2026, says its Suno litigation settled and announces a partnership for future licensed models and artist opt-in. [Suno's statement](https://suno.com/blog/wmg-partnership) confirms its partnership plan; [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/warner-music-group-settles-copyright-case-with-suno-licensed-ai-music-2025-11-25/) corroborates the settlement. These are not a finding that prior training was lawful or Universal/Sony settled.
- 5 June 2026 is the last corroborated status for Universal in [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/musicians-union-sues-record-labels-over-ai-licensing-2026-06-05/), which described its Suno case as ongoing then. A [newer filing indexed for 18 September 2026](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/files/2026/09/26-cv-14275-Dkt.-1-Complaint.pdf) was inaccessible (403); no authoritative current docket was obtained. **Exact procedural status on 12 November 2026 is unverified.** Instructor should check docket before teaching any live update. No status/verdict is invented.
- Separate legal questions: permission to copy recordings for training; protectability of human expression in a new output; fact-specific resemblance of an output; ethical credit/compensation. The [US Copyright Office Part 2](https://www.copyright.gov/ai/Copyright-and-Artificial-Intelligence-Part-2-Copyrightability-Report.pdf) is US guidance about output copyrightability, not a Hong Kong rule or a ruling on Suno training. Real product releases need current jurisdiction-specific advice.
