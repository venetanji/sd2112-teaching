## 14 / Learning is selective. Is it unfair?

About two minutes within 25-45. Treat everything a network learns is bias as a provocation, not a technical definition. Distinguish inductive bias, learned regularity and social unfairness. Statistical bias is another sense: a systematic deviation relative to a specified target or estimator, not a synonym for all three. Mitchell (1980), The Need for Biases in Learning Generalizations, sections 1-4 and conclusion, defines the basis for preferring generalizations beyond consistency with examples. Application to neural architecture, representation, objective and optimization is our extension. Necessary selectivity does not excuse preventable discrimination. A model is not a complete or neutral picture of humanity. The source is linked on the reference shelf.
