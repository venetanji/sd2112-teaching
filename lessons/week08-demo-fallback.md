# Week 8 manual literature-search demonstration

Instructor preparation, not a new student submission. The entire demo occupies relative minutes **90-105**. No agent, Scopus abstract entitlement or new account is necessary. The live agent comparison is **not preflighted** and defaults to a manual verification exercise.

## Fifteen-minute route

1. **0-3: form a question.** For an illustrative AI rehearsal partner: when does preparation help a person initiate a difficult conversation? Combine system, task/context and outcome; vary synonyms. Do not search for a predetermined pro/con verdict.
2. **3-10: demonstrate manually.** Start at <https://www.lib.polyu.edu.hk/>. Use discovery or a relevant subject database available to the instructor. Show the actual interface, not invented menu instructions. Inspect a record's authors, title, date, article type and DOI; then open the primary source through legitimate library access or a lawful author manuscript. Show abstract versus methods/results, note access level, and follow references/citing papers. Filters narrow a search; they do not guarantee quality. Never project login credentials or personal records.
3. **10-15: verify one candidate.** Without an agent, use one of the cards below. If an instructor has independently preflighted an agent, ask for candidates, not a verdict. Verify DOI/title/authors/version, actual access, supporting passage/location and inference manually. A tool returning metadata has not read the abstract or paper. If it fails, continue the same manual card.

## Network/login fallback

- Open R8 directly: <https://www.nature.com/articles/s44271-025-00387-3>. It is open access; no Scopus abstract endpoint is required. A discovery outage does not prevent showing the record, full-text sections and references.
- R6's final primary PDF is also publicly author-hosted: <https://www.hbs.edu/ris/Publication%20Files/AI%20Companions%20Reduce%20Loneliness%2011.7.2025_57451c02-8047-4e0d-abfc-55841f64166d.pdf>.
- Before class, open the two evidence ledgers and this file locally. If the network is entirely unavailable, use these **prepared verification examples**, explicitly not a live search or a systematic review. Do not claim that offline demonstration proves current library service availability.
- Teams without access can formulate queries, inspect these examples and record candidate references; mark their own access limits and finish lawful retrieval later. Do not fabricate full-text methods to complete a card.

## Prepared card A: source choice is not response quality

**Record:** Wenger, J. D., Cameron, C. D., & Inzlicht, M. (2026). *People choose to receive human empathy despite rating AI empathy higher*. Communications Psychology, 4, 19. <https://doi.org/10.1038/s44271-025-00387-3>.

**Access used for deck:** full published HTML, not abstract only. See `week08-evidence-benefits.md`, R8, for locators.

**Question:** does liking the language imply choosing the AI as the source?

**Method/comparator/outcome:** four brief nonclinical studies; 691 source-choice participants across studies; choice of human versus AI empathy and ratings of experienced responses. Study 4 uses consequential choices and later ratings. Source selection is chosen, not randomized for rating comparisons.

**Finding:** preference for a human source can coexist with higher ratings for experienced AI responses. This is neither unanimity nor a clinical outcome.

**Where to inspect live:** Figure 1 caption for study sample sizes; Study 4 Results for source choice; General Discussion, Limitations for brief/decontextualized sources and transfer gaps. Do not invent a PDF page for the HTML.

**Transfer prompt:** our rehearsal product may support a later human conversation, rather than replace its source. That is our hypothesis, not an outcome tested by R8. What would we need to compare with intended users?

## Prepared card B: relief does not imply persistence

**Record:** De Freitas, J., Oguz-Uguralp, Z., Uguralp, A. K., & Puntoni, S. (2026; first online 2025). *AI companions reduce loneliness*. Journal of Consumer Research, 52(6), 1126-1148. <https://doi.org/10.1093/jcr/ucaf040>.

**Version/access:** final five-study published article, author-hosted PDF reviewed in full. Do not substitute the older six-study working paper or its numbering.

**Method/comparator/outcome:** Study 3, seven days; prompted GPT-4 companion interactions, loneliness measured before/after versus no-interaction control ratings. This is different from the article's correlational app-review analysis.

**Exact short evidence locator:** General Discussion, PDF position 18: effects "did not accumulate or persist in the absence of interaction". PDF position 19 distinguishes temporary relief from enduring change. PDF position 14 reports no significant control versus before-experience differences across the seven days. PDF positions in the advance author-hosted article are not the journal's later 1126-1148 pagination.

**Finding/limit:** immediate self-reported relief, not sustained improvement between sessions; brief US setting and differential attrition matter. Not a clinical recommendation or replacement for human relationships.

**Product hypothesis:** measure a useful outcome beyond time in chat. Do not convert a transient effect into a claim of durable treatment. Ask what evidence would change the team's design decision.

## Instructor safety and access contract

- No private team/user data in search or agent prompts; use a fictional example.
- No gated-library automation, credential display or access-control bypass.
- Keep DOI verification, article access and claim verification as separate steps.
- Do not redistribute publisher PDFs in this repository. Students follow primary links with their own legitimate access.
- Save references and evidence cards in ordinary team notes; no new Canvas/ClassPoint upload. The existing formal Week 8 proposal and its actual Canvas deadline remain in force.
