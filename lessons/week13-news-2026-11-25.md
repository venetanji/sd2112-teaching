# Week 13 · Optional AI news references (25 November 2026)

### Kyutai AURA · 20 November 2026

Kyutai released code, model weights and a demo for turning a portrait and streaming speech into a talking head. The authors report that the 1.3B version meets its 576p/16 fps playback target on an H100; the 5B version produces 720p frames but does not meet its 25 fps playback target on one H100. Both weight licenses restrict commercial use. The performance claims are from the authors, not measurements made in class. [Announcement and samples](https://kyutai.org/blog/2026-11-20-aura/) · [Model table and licenses](https://github.com/kyutai-labs/aura#models)

**Ask:** Using the published samples, where would you put the disclosure in a product that animates a portrait, and what consent would the product need? No live microphone or portrait upload is required.

### Ideogram 4.6 · 19 November 2026

Ideogram released Generate and Precise Edit through its API. Precise Edit takes an optional mask; supported output is up to 4K, with the Very low setting capped at 2K. The vendor’s reported benchmark rank is not evidence that a particular poster’s text, grid or logo will survive an edit. [Ideogram 4.6 model and API notes](https://ideogram.ai/models/4.6/)

**Ask:** In a before/after pair made with a fictional poster, what was intended to change and what must stay identical? Inspect the unmasked area for drift before judging polish. A prepared example is enough; no paid API call.
